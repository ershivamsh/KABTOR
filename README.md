# SortAI — AI-Powered E-Waste Sorting and Recycling Marketplace

**SortAI** is an AI-powered e-waste management platform that helps informal scrap collectors (kabadiwalas) identify, segregate, and sell electronic waste more efficiently and safely.

Today, many scrap collectors lack the technical knowledge to distinguish valuable electronic components from ordinary scrap or recognise potentially hazardous materials. This leads to mixed waste, lost economic value, unsafe handling, and difficulty connecting with specialised recyclers.

SortAI addresses this problem through a smartphone-based computer vision system that identifies electronic waste from images and recommends the appropriate recycling category. It guides collectors using a simple, colour-coded interface with visual instructions and multilingual voice assistance. Potentially hazardous or uncertain items are flagged for review rather than being automatically classified as safe.

The platform also includes a digital inventory system that tracks sorted materials in category-specific crates. When collected material reaches a predefined pickup threshold, SortAI connects the collector with eligible recyclers through a B2B marketplace where recyclers can submit competing bids for available material lots. Collectors can compare offers, arrange pickups, record material quality, and track transactions.

SortAI aims to transform fragmented, informal e-waste collection into a safer, more organised, and economically transparent recycling supply chain.

**Key innovations:**
- Computer vision for e-waste identification and categorisation.
- Safety-first classification with an Unknown/Needs Review workflow.
- Colour-coded sorting instructions designed for accessibility and low-literacy users.
- Inventory tracking and threshold-based pickup requests.
- A category-specific recycler marketplace with competitive bidding.
- An offline-capable AI architecture for use in areas with limited connectivity.

**Expected impact:** SortAI aims to reduce material contamination, improve access to appropriate recycling channels, support better-value sales for scrap collectors, and encourage safer electronic waste management.

The project combines artificial intelligence, accessibility, sustainability, and marketplace technology to make responsible e-waste recycling more practical and accessible.
